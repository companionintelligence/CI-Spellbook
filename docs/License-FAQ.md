# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what Companion Spellbook's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute Companion Spellbook source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

Companion Spellbook is a curated catalog of recommended agents, apps, and tools for the Companion Intelligence ecosystem.

Companion Spellbook uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Spellbook user?

If you use Companion Spellbook for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork Companion Spellbook?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute Companion Spellbook under the same license.

### Can I contribute to Companion Spellbook?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell Companion Spellbook?

No. Selling Companion Spellbook, including paid placement or paid access to the catalog, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Most other Companion Intelligence product repositories use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories — see the org's open-source rollout tracker for the current list.

### Unsure whether your use case is allowed?

Email support@companionintelligence.com.
